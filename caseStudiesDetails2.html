In cooperation with the Palestinian Red Crescent Society

In collaboration with the Palestinian Red Crescent Society, ETA implemented one of three desalination plants at Al-Quds Hospital, with a production capacity of 30,000 liters per day per unit, aiming to provide a sustainable and safe source of clean water for patients and medical staff on the front lines.
