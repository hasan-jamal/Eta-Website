ETA – A Subsidiary of Abdul Salam Yaseen Holding Company

ETA is a leading company specialized in providing sustainable and integrated solutions in the field of water purification and distribution. As a subsidiary of Abdul Salam Yaseen & Sons Holding Company, ETA is committed to delivering high-quality water solutions that safeguard public health and support environmental sustainability. Since its establishment, ETA has built a strong reputation through its expertise, efficiency, and customer-focused approach.
