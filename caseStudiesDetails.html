12 Feb 2024

The company Abdul Salam Yassine completed the project of manufacturing and supplying a water desalination plant with a capacity of 10,000 liters per day.

As part of our commitment to providing innovative solutions for sustainable development and in response to the humanitarian consequences of the war in Gaza, Abd Al-Salam Yaseen Company has successfully completed the manufacturing and supply of a 10,000-liter-per-day water desalination plant at its facility in Mawasi Khan Younis to support the European Hospital. This project was funded by the International Committee of the Red Cross, reaffirming our role in supporting the local healthcare sector.
